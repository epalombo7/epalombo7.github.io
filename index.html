Edoardo Palombo, PhD

Economist, World Bank · Macroeconomics · Financial markets · Sub-Saharan Africa

I'm the Sub-Saharan Africa regional economist in the World Bank's Prospects Group, where I write the region's outlook in the Global Economic Prospects report. I also carry out economic research on growth, monetary policy, financial markets and development.

Research Policy work CV

About

I've spent over 12 years across international organizations, the private sector and academia. Before the World Bank, I was an economist at Deloitte in London, advising business leaders on the global outlook, and I taught macroeconomics at Queen Mary University of London, where I earned my PhD.

My research covers macroeconomics, financial markets, monetary and fiscal policy, uncertainty and climate change. It has been published in the American Economic Review: Insights and cited by the Bank of England and the IMF.
